Hexacopter Drone

Purpose

The main goal of this project was to measure wrench data from a hexacopter drone in different positions. Thus a specialized hexacopter had to be designed and bulit to accomidate these goals. It had to mount 6 motors, house all required electronics, and withstand structural stress from testing.

Drone Model

The drone was modeled in SolidWorks. This included designing custom parts such as the mounting adapters and acrylic plate that let the drone mount directly to the UR5 arm. The arms of the hexacopter were DJI's F450 frame arms. All components were assembled into one SolidWorks assembly (Shown in the image to the left).

FEA Analysis

An FEA analysis was conducted on every part individually and together as an assembly to ensure they could withstand the forces experianced by the drone.

Hexacopter Construction

All parts were assembled with some alterations to the original design. The bottom acrylic plate was swaped for a DJI haxacopter plate with interal electrical connection points.

Hexacopter Electronics

There was quite a large amount of electrical equipment. For flight, there were 6 motors, 6 ESCs, battery connector, and a radio transmitter. Along with all the nessessary flight components were the electronics needed for testing. This included a transmitter, PX4 based control module, and USB XRCE bridge module (See by opening "MORE PICTURES" below and scrolling down). With a great amount of soldering and wairing later, the drone was now complete.
